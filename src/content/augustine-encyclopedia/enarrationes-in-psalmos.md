  - "Expositions on the Book of Psalms, 6 vols., in the series Library of the Fathers (Oxford: Parker, 1853–57), offers a stiff but reliable translation. The abridgement of that work in A Select Library of the Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers, First Series, vol. 8 (Boston: Christian Literature Company, 1886; many reprints) unfortunately changes the Maurist section numbers, making comparison with the Latin difficult. Of only limited use for study is the loose translation of the homilies on Psalms 1–37, On the Psalms, vols. 29 and 30 in the Ancient Christian Writers series (New York: Newman, 1960–61). A new translation of the Enarrationes is in preparation for the Augustinian Heritage Institute series, The Works of Saint Augustine."
